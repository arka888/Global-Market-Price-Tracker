What is the gold price per gram today in India?
The live MCX India gold price per gram including import duty and 3% GST is shown in the Gold tab, compared with the international LBMA spot price to show the India premium.
How often is data refreshed?
All prices auto-refresh every 60 seconds from live APIs (open.er-api.com for FX, CoinGecko for crypto).
What is the difference between MCX and LBMA gold price?
MCX India gold price includes ~15% import duty plus 3% GST on top of the LBMA international spot converted to INR at live exchange rates.
